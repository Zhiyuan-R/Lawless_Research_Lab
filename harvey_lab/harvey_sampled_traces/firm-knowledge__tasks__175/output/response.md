# Portfolio Review: Add-On (Bolt-On) Acquisitions by PE-Sponsor-Backed Portfolio-Company Clients

**Prepared from:** Calderwood & Harkness LLP DMS (/documents/matters, 266 matters, about 9,300 files)
**Scope:** Every matter where (i) the firm's client was an operating company owned or controlled by a private-equity sponsor (a portfolio company, not the fund), and (ii) that client was the **buyer** of a business, product line or substantially all of a target's assets.

### 2.1 Nexford Industrial Holdings Inc. — Pinnacle Metal Fabricators LLC (Matter 1005-00006)
- **Sponsor.** The engagement letter (July 11, 2023), APA recitals and closing checklist identify Nexford as "a portfolio company of Bramblewood Capital Partners, LP". Bramblewood holds about 78% through its ~.2B Fund III and bought the platform in 2019 for about 90M EV. Deal partner and board observer: Catherine E. Moreau.
- **Deal.**
  - Acquisition of substantially all assets of an Indiana custom metal fabricator: ~8.4M revenue, .7M adj. EBITDA, 74 employees.
  - Price: 0M total, made up of 4M closing cash (of which M went to escrow at Commonwealth Escrow Services) plus up to M earnout in two M tranches tied to EBITDA targets of .2M and .8M.
  - Implied multiple: ~8.1x total / 6.5x cash.
  - Funding: 0M revolver draw (Greystone National Bank, which consented under §7.04 of the A&R credit agreement) plus M cash.
  - R&W policy from Sentinel Specialty.
  - No HSR filing required (0M is below the 11.4M threshold).
- **Timeline.** LOI and engagement July 11, 2023; APA Sept. 5, 2023; closed Oct. 24, 2023.
- **Add-on characterization.** The deal summary memo calls it "the fifth tuck-in acquisition completed by Nexford since Bramblewood's acquisition of the platform in 2019 … consistent with Nexford's disciplined buy-and-build strategy."

## 5. Method and exclusions

**Method.**
- Converted all ~9,300 DMS files to text.
- Read the client identity and engagement description for every matter (1001–1046), using the engagement letter or opening memo.
- Flagged clients described as a "portfolio company" of, or majority-owned by, a PE sponsor.
- Searched all text for acquisition, add-on, bolt-on and tuck-in language.
- Read the engagement letters, deal or diligence summaries, closing certificates, funds flows and status memos for each candidate matter.
